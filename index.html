Inkfold

Write, type and speak your notes. The intelligence stays on your iPhone. Coming soon to the App Store.

One place for every kind of note

  • Write with Apple Pencil. Your handwriting is read on your iPhone, so you can search it like typed text and convert a passage when you want to.
  • Record while you write. A transcript appears as people talk. Tap a word, or a line you wrote at that moment, to hear what was said.
  • Apple Intelligence on the device. Summaries, titles, tags, action items for Reminders and a meeting report with decisions and open questions. You check and keep what you want.
  • Ask your notes. Type a question in search and get a short answer with the notes it came from.
  • Practice. Turn a note into flashcards that come back on a schedule.
  • Write on PDFs, and share any note as PDF, Markdown or text.

Made for iPhone Duo

Closed, Inkfold is a notebook in your pocket with its tools along the side. Open it and your notes and the open note sit side by side, with the transcript next to them while you record.
