Privacy Policy

Inkfold doesn't collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing you write, type or say is sent to us.

Your notes, handwriting, recordings, transcripts, imported PDFs and flashcards are stored on your device.

Handwriting recognition, speech transcription, search and the Apple Intelligence features all run on your device with Apple's on-device models. Inkfold doesn't use cloud AI or Private Cloud Compute.

The microphone is used only while you record. Inkfold asks for access the first time you tap Record.

Reminders are only used when you choose to add action items to them. Inkfold asks for access the first time you do.

Notes appear in Spotlight search on your device. The Inkfold widget shows the titles of your latest notes and hides them while your iPhone is locked.

When you share a note, the file goes where you send it; that is your choice.
