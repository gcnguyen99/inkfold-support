Support

What do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 27 or later. Apple Pencil works on iPhone Duo and iPad. The Apple Intelligence features (summaries, meeting reports, flashcards, answers to questions) need an iPhone or iPad that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. Everything else works without it.

Which languages?

Inkfold reads handwriting and transcribes speech in the languages your iPhone supports for these, including English, Dutch, German, French and Spanish. It uses the languages set in Settings > General > Language & Region. Apple Intelligence answers in the language of your note.

My handwriting isn't found in search

Inkfold reads a page a few seconds after you stop writing. Neat, separate letters are read best; joined-up handwriting is read less reliably, especially in languages other than English.

Pen gestures

Strike a word through with one straight line, or scribble over it, to erase it. Undo brings it back. You can turn gestures off with Pen Gestures in the note's menu.

Where are my notes?

On your iPhone, in Inkfold's own storage. Recordings and imported PDFs are in the Inkfold folder in the Files app. To keep a copy elsewhere, share a note as PDF or Markdown.
